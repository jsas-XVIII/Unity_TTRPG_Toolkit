# Chapter III: Classes

## Class Overview

Nine unique classes. Each class defines:
- **Main Attribute** — used for Attack Rating (AR) and damage bonuses on all attacks
- **Starting HP** — base value + MIGHT modifier
- **Recuperation Die** — die type rolled to recover HP during Respite
- **Class Resource** — fuel for powers (with max pool size and recharge die)
- **Weapon/Armor Competencies**
- **Class Features** — passive baseline abilities everyone in the class has
- **Class Perks** — choose 1 from 2 options at Level 1; more via leveling
- **Class Powers** — Tier I (start with 3); at Level 5 choose 2 Tier II Powers for free (not drawn from tokens)

**Class Resources** recharge via:
- **Respite** or **Full Rest**
- **Rolling doubles** on Attack or Defense rolls in combat (roll recharge die to restore resources)

**Secondary Resources** (some classes only):
- Driftwalker: uses Blood (HP) + Bile (primary resource)
- Priest: uses Healing Charges (secondary, generated by using certain powers)
- Primalist: uses Spirit (primary) + Ferocity (secondary; Spirit-spending powers generate Ferocity)

## The Nine Classes

### 1. Dreadnought
*"An unstoppable fighter that thrives in the heat of battle."*

| Trait | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Main Attribute | MIGHT |
| Starting HP | 14 + MIGHT |
| Recuperation Die | d10 |
| Resource | Fury [max 6], Recharge: 1d4 |
| Armor Competency | Light, Heavy |
| Weapon Competency | Medium Melee, Heavy Melee |

**Class Features:**
- **Shieldbreaker** (Passive) — Melee attacks gain +1 Armor Penetration (ignore 1 AV); +2 at Level 5, +3 at Level 9
- **Cleave** (Standard) — 2 Fury; roll 1d4 to determine number of Adjacent enemies hit; roll Basic Attack; Success: 1d10+HL damage; Failure: half damage

**Playstyle:** Aggressive front-line melee; gains power as battle progresses; reckless, high-damage, ignores pain. Best supported by teammates who protect their flanks.

---

### 2. Driftwalker
*"An occult caster specializing in blood magicks and dark bargains."*

- Main Attribute: MIND
- Resources: Bile (primary) + Blood (HP; spent on some powers)
- Deals in dark bargains; powerful crowd control and debuffs
- Secondary resource (Blood) means Driftwalker powers can cost HP directly

---

### 3. Fell Hunter
*"A deadly marksman with heightened senses and an axe to grind with the demonic."*

- Main Attribute: AGILITY
- Ranged specialist; tracking and survival abilities
- Class Features include wilderness/tracking perks
- Particularly effective against Fell (demonic) enemies
- Works with Afflicted Tinkerers for specialized equipment

---

### 4. Judge
*"A righteous warrior powered by the Divine and fighting for justice."*

- Main Attribute: MIGHT (melee) / uses Fervor resource (Recharge: 1d4)
- Divine warrior; inspires allies; powerful presence in society (favorable NPC interactions)
- Dual wield longswords deals 2d6 (Medium→Heavy die increase)

---

### 5. Mystic
*"A student of the magickal arts that manipulates and bends reality to her will."*

- Main Attribute: MIND
- Resource: Mana (Recharge: 1d6)
- Pure caster; arcane damage in multiple elemental types (Frost, Corrosive, Electric, Arcane, etc.)
- Example power: **Acid Bolt** — 1d6+MIND+HL Corrosive damage + MIND Corrosive damage at start of target's turn for 2 rounds

---

### 6. Phantom
*"An agile fighter utilizing shadow and guile to strike with lethal impact."*

- Main Attribute: AGILITY
- Resource: Guile
- Light/medium melee; light armor; backstab damage bonuses
- Weapon Competency: Light and Medium Melee; Armor Competency: Light
- Excels when enemies are distracted or flanked
- Example power: **Tumble** (Reaction) — spend 1 Guile to reduce incoming damage by 3

---

### 7. Priest
*"A pious caster that wields her faith as a weapon while bolstering and healing allies."*

- Main Attribute: MIND
- Resources: Faith (primary) + Healing Charges (secondary; generated by using certain powers)
- Healer and support; can also deal Divine damage
- Healing Charges enable targeted healing powers
- Can cure Diseases
- All Priests choose a **Class Path** at character creation: **Chaplain** or **War Priest**

#### Class Path: Chaplain
*"You are focused on the strict study of holy texts and understanding the divine song that flows from your god to you. The healing arts and accepting your god's grace come easily to you."*

- 10 Faith points; recharge die: 1d6
- Can hold 4 Healing Charges
- Receives **Sacred Bolt** as a baseline power (Standard; 3 Faith; Single, Far)
  - *Success:* 1d6+HL+MIND Divine damage and gain 1 Healing Charge
  - *Failure:* Half damage and gain 1 Healing Charge

#### Class Path: War Priest
*"You are the physical embodiment of the fury of your god. You understand that in order to make your gospel heard by the heretic, you must get up close and personal. Your will is iron and your body is steel."*

- 6 Faith points; recharge die: 1d4
- Can hold 3 Healing Charges
- Can use Heavy Armour, Heavy Weapons, and Shields
- Receives **Holy Strike** as a baseline power (Standard; 2 Faith; Single, Adjacent)
  - *Success:* Basic Attack damage and gain 1 Healing Charge
  - *Failure:* Half Basic Attack damage and gain 1 Healing Charge

#### Healing Charge (Quick — all Priests)
*"The song of your god will mend that which is broken."* Target: Single, Far.
Each Healing Charge heals for 1+HL+MIND. Multiple charges may be spent on the same target at once.
You start with 1 Healing Charge; every Full Rest grants 1 Healing Charge.

---

### 8. Primalist
*"A hybrid warrior harnessing the power of nature to call down the elements and augment his body with the primal savagery of its beasts."*

- Main Attribute: Variable (nature caster + melee hybrid)
- Resources: Spirit (primary) + Ferocity (secondary)
- Spirit-spending powers generate Ferocity; Ferocity fuels physical augmentation powers
- Can switch between ranged elemental casting and close-quarters augmented melee

---

### 9. Sentinel
*"The perfect soldier that was designed to protect and defend."*

- Main Attribute: MIGHT
- Starting HP: 12 + MIGHT
- Recuperation Die: d10
- Resource: Discipline [max 8–10], Recharge: 1d4
- Armor Competency: Light, Heavy
- Weapon Competency: Medium Melee, Heavy Melee, Shields

**Class Features (example — Calista, sample character):**
- **Bastion** — bonus to Defense Rating
- **Vigilance** — situational awareness bonus
- **Back to Back** (power) — Sentinel and one Adjacent Ally roll Attack and Defense for each other, using the highest result; lasts 2 rounds

**Playstyle:** Defensive front-line; protects allies; high survivability; excels at controlling enemies near them.

## Reading Power Cards

```
[Power Name]                    [Action Type]
Cost: [Resource amount]   Target: [Single/AoE/etc.]   Range: [Adjacent/Nearby/Far/etc.]

EFFECTS
Roll your Basic Attack.
Success. [What happens on hit]
Failure. [What happens on miss — often half damage]

UPGRADES
[Upgrade Name]. [What the upgrade does]
```

### Action Types on Power Cards
- **STANDARD** — uses Standard action
- **QUICK** — uses Quick action
- **REACTION** — triggered by conditions, usable on GM's turn; once per round
- **MAINTAIN** — ongoing; reduce max Class Resource pool by activation cost while active
- **OVERDRIVE** — once per Full Rest; rechargeable via Adrenaline Rush
- **ULTIMATE** — once per Full Rest; NOT rechargeable via Adrenaline Rush
- **PASSIVE** — always on; no action required

### Range Terms in Powers
- **Adjacent** — within 2.5m
- **Nearby** — 2.5m to 8m
- **Far** — 8m to 16m
- **Very Far** — 16m to 24m
