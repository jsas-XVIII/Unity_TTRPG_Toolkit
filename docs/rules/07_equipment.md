# Chapter VII: Equipment

## Wealth & Currency

### The Denerim
- Standard currency across Unity
- Named after Deneri, wife of the Furian metalsmith who minted the first coin
- **Scale reference:** Hot meal + room at an inn = 10–15 Denerim; leather armor = ~70 Denerim

### Starting Wealth
- **150 Denerim** (difficult start) — barely covers armor, weapon, and basic gear
- **250 Denerim** (relaxed start) — allows second weapon set, lodging, bribes

### Selling
- Mundane weapons/armor: 20–50% of market value
- Jewelry, gems, rare metals, cultural items: retain or exceed market value
- Artifacts/magical items: very high value; can only be sold to wealthy/specialized buyers (not street merchants)
- Merchants have limited funds (GM sets their Denerim pool)

### Competency
- Any character can wear any armor/use any weapon
- Without Competency: suffer **Hindrance** on Attack rolls (weapons) or Defense rolls (armor)

## Dual Wielding

- Requires Competency one step above the two weapons being dual wielded
- **Effect:** Damage die increases by one step (no extra attacks, no additional bonuses)
  - Two Light Melee weapons → deal 1d8 (Medium die) instead of 1d6
  - Two Medium Melee weapons → deal 2d6 (Heavy die) instead of 1d8
- Without proper Competency: suffer Hindrance on every attack

## Weapon & Armour Customization

Players can request custom items from the GM (e.g., a dual-bladed glaive, a giant totem). Work with GM to assign an appropriate category. Unity's equipment rules prioritize character fantasy over rigid classification.

**Quick loadout suggestions (if not purchasing individually):**
- Sentinel: Medium weapon, Heavy armor, shield
- Mystic: Light melee weapon (or Light ranged weapon)
- Dreadnought: Heavy melee weapon(s), Heavy armor

## Gear & Necessities

**Necessities** are tracked as a consumable:
- Each **Respite** or **Full Rest** costs **1 Necessity** (includes food, water, camp supplies)
- Without Necessities: rest benefits are halved

**Gear** is a general resource for miscellaneous adventuring supplies:
- Used in some class powers (e.g., Afflicted Grisly Triage: spend 1 Gear to scavenge Recuperation)
- Represents tools, rope, torches, basic alchemical components, etc.

## Artifacts (Magical Items)

Artifacts emit a unique energy (a form of "radiation"). Equipping too many causes Hindrance on all actions.

### Artifact Limit by Level
| Level | Max Equipped Artifacts |
|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | 1 |
| + (per Advancement) | +1 (check class table for when) |

### Artifact Usage Notes
- Rare finds during adventures
- Some can only be sold to high-caliber buyers
- Provide unique powers, bonuses, or special effects

### Sample Artifact (from book)
**Essence Extractor** — Created by Afflicted Splicer Dr. Ecclair VonWaalden; extracts soul/memories of a living being and transplants them into an automaton. Requires life force to power. (Story/NPC item, not player equipment.)
