# Chapter VIII: Foes & Fiends

## Encounter Basics

### Danger Level (DL)
- Every enemy has a **Danger Level (DL)** analogous to a PC's Level
- DL = rough indicator of threat relative to party level
- DL is based on raw stats; powerful unique powers may increase effective DL (e.g., Shade's Incorporeal = +2 DL)

### Quantity vs. Quality
| Scenario | Enemy Count Guideline |
|----------|----------------------|
| DL ≈ Party Level | 1–2 more enemies than PCs |
| DL much higher OR using an Elite | 1 enemy vs. party; Elite at same DL or 1 higher |
| Swarm scenario (lower DL) | More enemies than PCs; pick creatures 1–3 DL below party |
| DL slightly higher than party | Match enemy count to PC count |

### Elite Enemies
- Vastly more powerful than standard enemies of same DL
- Can take on an entire 4-player party alone or with minimal support
- Purple header box on monster card (vs. red for standard monsters)
- Elite DL = roughly equivalent to a 4-player party of equal or 1-lower level

### Ruin Points & Monster Powers
- Most monster special abilities cost **Ruin** to activate
- GMs must spend accumulated Ruin to use these powers
- **Without Ruin mechanic:** treat Ruin cost as cooldown in rounds
  - Example: 2 Ruin cost = 2-round cooldown + any baseline recharge = 4-round total cooldown
  - Powers with no cooldown and 1 Ruin cost = 2-round cooldown

### Monster Death
- Monsters do NOT become Incapacitated at 0 HP — they die immediately
- Exception: if monster has a specific ability stating otherwise

### Monster Friendly Fire
- Monster AoE that hits their own allies: auto-hit (maximum damage or roll damage freely)
- Optional: players roll friendly fire damage; or assume max for speed
- Some AoE powers explicitly target "enemies only" — allies safe even if in range

## Monster Types & Sizes

### Types
- **Standard** — red header on monster card; normal enemies
- **Elite** — purple header; boss-level threats; can challenge entire party

### Sizes
Size affects some powers and contest rules:
- **Small, Medium, Large, Colossal** (general scale)
- Contesting vs. a Large+ or Elite target: must contest MIGHT vs. MIGHT (10 + target's MIGHT as TN)

---

## Reading Monster Cards

Monster cards (stat blocks) contain:
1. **Name & DL** — top header
2. **Size, Type, XP Value** — sub-header
3. **HP, AR, DR, MR, AV, SPD, DMG** — core stats
4. **Powers** — special abilities with Ruin costs and descriptions

## Fine-Tuning Difficulty On-the-Fly

| Adjustment Level | HP | AR | DR | DMG |
|------------------|----|----|----|-----|
| Minor | +20% | +1 | +2 | +2 |
| Moderate | +40% | +2 | +4 | +4 |
| Severe | +60% | +3 | +6 | +6 |

**Other levers:**
- **HP** — quickest fix for monsters dying too fast; slightly devalues elemental damage
- **AV / Resistances** — counters Physical-heavy or elemental-heavy parties
- **Narrative First Strike** — force enemies to go first without touching stats
- **Power usage** — using monster powers tactically amplifies their threat without changing stats

## Monster Creation Tips

1. **Start weak, scale up** — err on the weaker side and ramp difficulty gradually
2. **Re-skin existing monsters** — take a Zombie's explosive power, change damage type to Electric/Fire, put it in a robot
3. **Unique powers inflate DL** — a single very powerful ability can make a DL 3 creature fight like a DL 5+
4. **Adjust on the fly** — narrative fixes (force enemies to go first) are often easier and faster than recalculating stats mid-session
