# Chapter IX: GM Guide

## Philosophy

The GM's role is:
- **Creator** — builds the world, NPCs, and plot hooks
- **Narrator** — describes scenes and brings the world to life
- **Adjudicator** — rules on ambiguous situations

The GM is not the antagonist. The GM-player relationship is **collaborative** — the goal is shared memorable moments, not defeating the players.

**Key mindset:** The GM's power is a tool to generate a compelling, cooperative play experience. Push and pull tension at the right times to keep everyone riveted.

---

## Session Zero

A pre-campaign discussion with the full group:
- Establish what type of game everyone wants to play
- Set expectations for tone, themes, content
- Allow players to inform their character-making decisions
- Give the GM clarity on player expectations for tailoring the campaign

---

## Character Creation Guidance (for GMs)

- Help players understand each class's role and playstyle
- Encourage group discussion of power synergies during power selection
- Recommend players read through Core Rules and Combat Rules before finalizing powers
- Suggest party compositions with balanced roles (tank, healer/support, damage, utility)

---

## Experience & Leveling Up

**XP is awarded from:**
- Defeating enemies (XP per monster on its card; see [08_foes_fiends.md](08_foes_fiends.md))
- Completing objectives and milestones (GM discretion)

**Level cap:** Not explicitly capped in base rules; GM determines campaign scope.

**When leveling up:** Consult the character's Class Advancement Table for all bonuses gained at that level (HP Boost, Core Path Point, Attribute Boost, Tier Token, AR/DR increases, etc.).

---

## Player vs. Player (PvP)

Unity does not by default support PvP. However, some situations may arise naturally:
- **Confused** status effect can cause a PC to attack another PC — resolve normally
- If groups agree to PvP scenarios: both sides roll as normal (attacker rolls Attack, defender rolls Defense)
- Recommended to handle with care — focus on story consequences over mechanical outcomes

---

## Adventuring

**Pacing:** Balance combat, exploration, and social encounters. The world is dangerous but there are still hidden gems and places of beauty.

**Narrative triggers:** Use environmental storytelling. The Drift bleeds into reality; strange anomalies, ruin energy, and Fell signs can foreshadow encounters.

**Failing Forward in the GM's hands:** When a player barely fails an out-of-combat roll, consider:
1. Partial success with a complication
2. Success with an unforeseen consequence
3. Failure that opens a new narrative thread
4. Only apply when it makes sense; don't force it for trivial tasks

## Ruin (GM Resource — Usage Guide)

### Accumulation
- Resting generates Ruin (Respite: +3 / Full Rest: +7)
- Player actions that are unsavory, dangerous, or push their luck generate Ruin
- Stored between sessions

### Spending Ruin
| Effect | Ruin Cost | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Monster Power | Varies (per power card) | Check monster's power list |
| Breach (Fell tear) | High | Reality tears; Fell pour through |
| Forced Intrusion | 3–5 (GM discretion) | Guarantee rest is interrupted |
| Empowered Encounter | 2–3 | Add extra enemies mid-encounter |
| Life is Difficult | 1–2 | Hostile NPC, rusted lock, bad day |

### Without Ruin Mechanic
If your group opts not to use Ruin: treat all Ruin costs as additional cooldown rounds on monster powers. A power with 2 Ruin cost + 2-round recharge = usable once every 4 rounds total.

## Spark Points (GM Perspective)

- Award Spark Points when players vividly describe their actions
- Adjust the Spark Die (capacity) and Moment of Glory threshold to balance difficulty
- A very imaginative group may accumulate Spark Points quickly — raise the threshold to maintain challenge

---

## Intrusions (GM Perspective)

When players take a Full Rest in an unsecured area:
1. Have a party member roll 1d10
2. Pre-declare 2 numbers (1–10) — if rolled, Intrusion triggers
3. Design Intrusion encounters below party level to avoid a full wipe on a tired party
4. Glass half full: intruders can carry Denerim (bandits) or become food (a bear = free Necessity recovery)

---

## Combat (GM Perspective)

### Default: GM Does Not Roll Dice
The default rule frees the GM from rolling — they focus on narration while player dice results flow naturally. Combat is one cohesive scene rather than individual exchanges.

### GM Dice Rolling (Optional)
If the GM wishes to roll:
- Use 2d10 + Monster's relevant Attribute for attacks and defenses
- See optional GM dice rolling rules in the Combat section
- Trade-off: slightly less narrative cohesion for the satisfaction of rolling

### Players Hesitant to Act (No Defined Turns)
Since Unity uses simultaneous team turns, players may be unsure when to act:
- Remind players all can discuss and act in any order
- Apply light time pressure narratively ("the enemy is advancing — what do you do?")
- Give a 10-second verbal count if needed; unanswered = forfeit turn

### Managing First Strike Advantage
First Strike is a major tactical advantage:
- If players keep winning First Strike and trivializing encounters: narratively force enemies first, or increase HP/DR slightly
- If enemies keep going first and hurting the party too much: reduce enemy AR/DMG slightly

---

## Distances (GM Notes)

When running combat without a grid, establish distances verbally at the start:
- Clearly state how far each combatant is from each other using the band terminology (Adjacent/Nearby/Far/Very Far)
- When misjudgment occurs over distance: **rule in favor of the players** — let them pull off the cool move; you can always add more enemies
- Players should always be encouraged to ask when uncertain about distance

---

## GM Dice Rolling (Optional Rules Reference)

If opting into full GM dice rolling for a campaign:
- Monsters roll 2d10 + AR for attacks (vs. player's DR as TN)
- Players roll 2d10 + DR to defend (vs. monster's AR as TN) — or the GM sets TN = monster AR
- Contesting NPCs: GM rolls 2d10 + Contested Attribute for NPC vs. player's result
- Monster AoE friendly fire defense: 2d10 + Monster's AGILITY vs. attacking teammate's AR

Default recommendation: Keep GM not rolling for smoother play, especially for new groups.
