# Chapter I: Welcome to Unity — World Lore

## Setting Summary

Unity is a post-apocalyptic fantasy world in the **Age of Wrath**. The once-benevolent Skyfather became the **Mad God** and sundered reality, merging the physical world with the **Drift** (a dimension of psychic/emotional/spiritual energy). This caused:
- The Great Calamity — the Skyfather's strike tore reality, unleashing Fell demons
- The races' great civilizations were shattered
- Automata (robots) gained sentience → the **Children of Steel**
- The dead rose → **The Risen**
- Ancient creatures re-emerged from the wilds
- The **Fell** (demonic hordes) poured through the **Tempest of Terror** portal in the **Dreadmarsh**

Each of the three primary races was cursed by the Skyfather as punishment for challenging/causing harm to his Ivory Queen.

---

## Themes

1. **A World on Fire** — Survival over progress; dark times, scarce resources, dangerous everywhere
2. **Moral Quagmire** — Questions of right and wrong in desperate times; sentient automata rights; grey areas
3. **Culture Clash** — Three races blame each other for the Calamity; racial tensions run high
4. **Technological Wonders** — Lost Golden Age tech (Titan Rigs, automata, arcane-steam constructs)
5. **Exploration & Discovery** — Hidden places of beauty and safety still exist; vast uninhabited territories

## Major Races (Playable)

### Valla
- Firstborn; elven-like; spirit stones in foreheads grant psychic connection (severed by Calamity)
- Long lifespans (centuries); slow to rouse; graceful
- Curse: **The Severing** — spirit stone link destroyed, plunging them into isolation and grief

### Furian
- Secondborn; volcanic origin; massive, lion-featured; industrious forgers
- Reservoir of inner power (the star-spark from the Ivory Queen)
- Curse: **The Red Rage** — tapping inner power with anger risks berserker madness; attacks allies uncontrollably

### Human
- Thirdborn; shortest lifespan; most adaptable; most technologically innovative
- Built automata, Titan Rigs; spread across all territories
- Curse: The **Phage** (afflicted upon their brightest minds) — see Afflicted below

### Afflicted
- Former Humans cursed with the **Phage** — a disease that eats flesh and bone
- Gradually replace lost parts with machinery; some fully embrace their hybrid form
- Ostracized, driven underground; brilliant minds; ruthless survivors
- Half-machine, half-human; organized into underground communities (castes: Splicers, Dusters, Exiles, etc.)

---

## Major Factions (Non-Playable Races)

### The Crimson Horde
- Ancient enemy of all three races; driven into the Great Wilds; re-emerging after the Calamity
- Various sub-races (Orcs, Goblins, Ragehounds, etc.)

### The Risen
- Undead; rose when Drift energy leaked into burial grounds
- Driven by **The Hunger** — absorbing memories and life force from the living
- Types: Vampires, Shades, Zombies, etc.
- The Risen retain varying amounts of their former memories

### Children of Steel
- Sentient automata who gained consciousness during the Great Calamity
- Led by **Alpharion**, the Machine King; seek freedom and liberation of enslaved automata
- Primary territory: the Iron Expanse (south)

### The Fell
- Demonic entities from the Drift; poured through the Tempest of Terror portal
- Driven by corruption; cannot be reasoned with
- Active in the **Dreadmarsh** near the Tempest of Terror

## The Drift

The Drift is a mirrored reality — a dimension composed of psychic, emotional, and spiritual energies of all sentient creatures. Before the Calamity, it was invisible and separate. After the Skyfather's strike, it bleeds into physical reality through cracks in the barrier, manifesting as:
- The Fell (demons that cross through)
- Ruin (bad luck, misfortune energy)
- Supernatural anomalies
- Sentience in automata
- The rising of the dead

The barrier between the physical world and the Drift remains fragile and continues to wane.
