# Chapter VI: Colossal Combat — Titan Rigs

## Overview

Titan Rigs are ancient mechanical giants from the Golden Age, built when all three races combined their gifts. They are:
- Enormous in scale (some said to touch the sky)
- Powered by depleted or poorly-charged power cores (deliberately limited by lore)
- A **collaborative experience** — operated by the whole party together
- Rare, short-lived encounters in a campaign; not permanent transportation

**Design intent:** Titan Rig play is narrative-first, mechanics are intentionally loose. It's meant to be chaotic, fun, and impactful — not the central focus of a session.

---

## Piloting a Titan Rig

### Party Size
- Designed for 4 players; adaptable for 2–5

### Key Difference from Normal Play
- Individual character rolls are replaced by a **collaborative Success Ladder**
- Main Attributes do NOT apply to Titan Rig actions
- Half Level + other bonuses apply instead

### Success Roll Formula
```
2d10 + Half Level + Intuition + Core Path Points + Bonuses
```
- Higher Level = more competency in interpreting ancient machinery
- Core Paths may grant partial bonuses at GM's discretion (e.g., "Inventor's Household" or "Pilot")
- Each player rolls and the results are tallied for the Success Ladder

---

## Success Ladder

| Successes | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| 0–1 | What you wanted does NOT happen; something bad happens instead |
| 2 | What you wanted happens, but you pay a narrative or mechanical price |
| 3–4 | You successfully achieve what you were attempting |

**What counts as a Success?** Matching or exceeding the GM-set TN (from the standard Difficulty Table, pg. 253 / see [04_core_rules.md](04_core_rules.md)) on your individual roll.

---

## Titan Rig Combat

### Basic Mechanics
- Shares core/combat rules at a macro scale
- Enemies: Colossal Monsters or other Titan Rigs
- Attacks and defenses follow the same roll structure but use Titan Rig stats instead of character stats

### Power Cells (Titan Rig Power)
- Everything a Titan Rig does consumes Power
- Power is a limited resource — cells are old and poorly charged
- Lore intentionally restricts how long Rigs can operate (keeps them from dominating a campaign)
- Power management is a tactical element of Titan Rig combat

### Structural Damage
- Titan Rigs can suffer Structural Damage from attacks
- Damage to specific systems may disable certain functions

## Colossal Monsters

Some encounters pit the party's Titan Rig against a massive creature rather than another machine. Colossal Monsters operate similarly to Titan Rigs from the GM side.

**Note on re-routing power:** Players can re-route Titan Rig Power mid-combat to gain tactical advantages — boosting offense at the cost of defense, or vice versa (exact mechanics are narrative/GM-driven based on the loose design intent).

---

## GM Notes

- Play loose; improvise freely
- Focus on fun and interesting situations over rules precision
- The power core limitation is a narrative tool — lean into it
- Titan Rigs change the scale of threats dramatically; enemies that were impossible become trivial (by design, for that feel of discovery and power)
